# Brand Context — Nexus Agriscience

## Parent Company: Nexus Agriscience

Nexus Agriscience is the parent company operating two distinct terpene brands. Based in Livermore, California, Nexus runs cultivation and extraction facilities producing botanical terpenes from real cannabis and hemp plants (not synthetic). The company is led by CEO Dalen, and is one of the fastest-growing hemp companies in the industry.

**Key facts:**
- Location: Livermore, CA (cultivation + extraction)
- Products: Botanical terpene profiles derived from actual cannabis/hemp cultivars
- Differentiator: Real plant-derived terpenes vs. synthetic or food-grade alternatives
- Compliance: Full COAs (Certificates of Analysis) on every batch
- Scale: Growing rapidly, expanding into new markets beyond cannabis

## Brand 1: Terpene Belt Farms (TBF)

**Website**: terpenebeltfarms.com
**Positioning**: The premium, science-backed botanical terpene supplier for serious manufacturers.

**Voice & Tone:**
- Professional, consultative, knowledgeable
- Lead with education, not sales pressure
- Speak like a trusted ingredient supplier, not a cannabis brand
- Use data, COAs, and case studies to build credibility
- Think: "Your R&D team's favorite supplier"

**Color Palette:**
- TBF Green (#2D5016 range) — earth tones, premium dark aesthetic
- Clean, professional typography
- Science/agriculture imagery

**Target Customers:**
1. Licensed cannabis manufacturers (edibles, vapes, concentrates, topicals)
2. CPG food & beverage companies exploring natural botanical flavors
3. Cosmetics and skincare formulators
4. Pharmaceutical R&D teams
5. Large MSOs needing consistent, high-volume terpene supply

**Key Messaging Pillars:**
- "Grown, not synthesized" — real botanical source material
- Batch consistency and full traceability
- Compliance-ready documentation for regulated industries
- Custom profile development for enterprise clients
- Education: what are terpenes, entourage effect, formulation best practices

**Common Objections & Responses:**
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "We already have a terpene supplier" | "Most manufacturers work with 2-3 suppliers for redundancy. We'd love to send samples for a side-by-side comparison." |
| "Botanical terpenes are all the same" | "Our profiles are derived from actual cannabis cultivars, not generic essential oil blends. The difference shows up in lab testing and consumer feedback." |
| "Too expensive" | "Our cost per unit decreases significantly at volume. Let's talk about your production scale and we'll build a custom pricing structure." |
| "We use cannabis-derived terpenes" | "Botanical terpenes offer batch-to-batch consistency that CDTs can't match, plus they're compliant in all 50 states. Many of our clients use both." |

## Brand 2: Duty Free Terpenes (DFT)

**Website**: dutyfreetrps.com
**Positioning**: Terpenes by extractors, for extractors. No gatekeeping, no corporate BS.

**Voice & Tone:**
- Rebellious, irreverent, anti-establishment
- Creator-to-creator energy
- Casual, direct, sometimes edgy
- Think: "Your homie who happens to run a terpene company"
- Uses slang naturally but never forced

**Color Palette:**
- Black and red — bold, aggressive
- Grunge/street aesthetic
- Cannabis culture imagery

**Target Customers:**
1. Small-batch cannabis extractors and concentrate artists
2. Home processors and hobbyists
3. Vape cartridge brands (small to mid-size)
4. Hemp/CBD product makers
5. Extract brands wanting strain-specific profiles

**Key Messaging Pillars:**
- "Same fire, no minimums" — same source material as TBF, accessible to small operators
- No gatekeeping: education, pricing, and access for everyone
- Strain-specific profiles that actually taste like the strain
- Fast shipping, no MOQ hassles
- Community: spotlight extract artists, share their work

**Common Objections & Responses:**
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I make my own terps / I use CDTs" | "Respect. But if you ever want consistent profiles for a product line, we're here. Same plant, every time." |
| "Never heard of you" | "We've been quiet on purpose. Same source as Terpene Belt Farms — just packaged for people like us." |
| "Too expensive for my scale" | "No minimums. Grab a sample kit and see for yourself. If the terps don't speak for themselves, no hard feelings." |
| "Terpenes are terpenes" | "Run a blind test with your crew. If you can't tell the difference, we'll shut up. But you will." |

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Positioning | Weakness vs. Nexus |
|---|---|---|
| Abstrax Tech | Cannabis-derived terpene leader, science-heavy | Higher price point, less accessible for small ops |
| True Terpenes | Mass-market botanical terpenes | Food-grade/synthetic blends, not from real cannabis cultivars |
| Floraplex | Budget botanical terpenes | Quality inconsistency, generic profiles |
| TerpFX | Mid-market botanical | Limited strain variety, smaller operation |
| Peak Supply Co | Budget-friendly, direct-to-consumer | Quality questions, no cultivation facility |

**Nexus's unfair advantage**: Owns the cultivation facility. Grows the actual plants. Most competitors buy bulk essential oils and blend to mimic cannabis profiles. Nexus extracts from real cannabis/hemp cultivars for authentic terpene profiles.

## Cannabis Industry Context for Prospecting

**Key industry events for lead generation:**
- MJBizCon (Las Vegas, annual — largest cannabis trade show)
- Hall of Flowers (California, biannual)
- Benzinga Cannabis Capital Conference
- Cannabis Science Conference
- NCIA events
- State-specific trade shows (Oklahoma, Michigan, etc.)

**Market dynamics to reference in outreach:**
- Terpene market growing as consumers demand flavor/experience differentiation
- Brands moving from THC potency wars → terpene-driven product positioning
- Regulatory trends pushing toward tested, documented ingredients
- New markets opening (Ohio, Maryland recently went recreational)
- Hemp/Farm Bill creating 50-state opportunities for botanical terpenes
