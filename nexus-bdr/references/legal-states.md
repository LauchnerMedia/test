## Notes for Prospecting

- **TBF outreach**: Focus on Tier 1 and Tier 2 states where licensed manufacturers operate
- **DFT outreach**: All 50 states for hemp/botanical products; Tier 1 for cannabis-specific products
- **Compliance**: Never reference THC content in outreach emails. Focus on "botanical terpenes" positioning
- **New market opportunity**: Ohio and Maryland recently went adult-use — manufacturers are scaling up and need suppliers. Prioritize these states for both brands.
- **Oklahoma**: Unique market — very low barrier to entry, thousands of small operators. Perfect for DFT.
- **New York**: Slow rollout but massive market potential. Large MSOs positioning early.
