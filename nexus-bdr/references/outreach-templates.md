## tbf_consultative

### Email 1 — Day 0: Intro + Value Hook

**Subject line formula**: `[Specific observation] + terpene sourcing`

Examples:
- "Saw your new live resin line — quick thought on profiles"
- "Your extraction team might find this useful"
- "[Company] + botanical terpene consistency"

**Body structure**:
```
Hi [First Name],

[1 sentence referencing something specific about their company — new product, job posting, funding, trade show appearance, social media post].

I'm reaching out from Terpene Belt Farms — we grow and extract botanical terpene profiles from actual cannabis cultivars at our facility in Livermore, CA. [1 sentence connecting their specific situation to how TBF helps].

Most of our manufacturing partners tell us the two things that matter most are batch consistency and compliance documentation. We provide full COAs on every batch and can develop custom profiles matched to your product line.

Would it make sense to send over a sample kit so your formulation team can test against what you're currently using?

[Your Name]
Terpene Belt Farms
```

### Email 2 — Day 7: Value-Add Follow-Up

**Subject**: "Re: [Original subject]" (keep in thread)

```
Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note last week. Wanted to share something your team might find useful —

[Insert one of: link to TBF blog post about terpene formulation, a relevant case study, an industry report about terpene market trends, or a COA example].

No pressure at all. If terpene sourcing is on your roadmap for this year, happy to be a resource.

[Your Name]
```

### Email 3 — Day 14: Soft Break-Up

**Subject**: "Closing the loop"

```
Hi [First Name],

I've reached out a couple times and want to respect your time. Totally understand if the timing isn't right.

If botanical terpene sourcing comes up down the road, I'm easy to find. We work with [general social proof — "dozens of licensed manufacturers across X states"] and are always happy to send samples.

Wishing you and the [Company] team a great [quarter/season].

[Your Name]
```

## Personalization Requirements

**CRITICAL**: Every outreach email MUST include at least one of these personalization elements:
1. Reference to a specific product they make or recently launched
2. Reference to a job posting (e.g., "I see you're hiring a formulation chemist — sounds like you're scaling production")
3. Reference to a recent funding round or acquisition
4. Reference to a trade show they attended or spoke at
5. Reference to a social media post or content they published
6. Reference to a specific challenge their market segment faces

**NEVER send a template without personalization.** If you can't find a personalization hook, flag the lead for manual review instead of sending generic outreach.
